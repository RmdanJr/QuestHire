# Kiroween 2026 submission (source: kiroween.devpost.com, read Nov 12, 2026)

## Key facts
- **Deadline:** Friday, Dec 4, 2026, 2:00 PM Pacific Time. Submission period Oct 30 - Dec 4; only new projects started in that window are eligible.
- **Category: Costume Contest.** "Build any app but show us a haunting user interface that's polished and unforgettable. Bring in spooky design elements that enhance your app's function."
- Teams up to 3 (QuestHire is solo). Registered participants can request Kiro credits on the Resources tab.

## Judging criteria
1. **Potential Impact:** a real problem for a real audience; useful, intuitive, accessible.
2. **Technological Implementation:** how thoroughly and skillfully Kiro is used; variety of Kiro tools; experimentation.
3. **Design:** a complete, coherent, polished and intentional product, not a proof of concept.
4. **Innovation/Idea:** novel concept; unusual technologies or public resources (datasets) combined in a new way.
5. **Presentation:** the video shows the project working end to end; clear problem, audience and why it matters; memorable.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Public **GitHub** repo (not GitLab/Bitbucket) with all source, assets and instructions.
- [ ] Open-source license detectable in the repo's About section (MIT, already in place and detected by GitHub).
- [ ] `/.kiro` at the repo root and **not** in `.gitignore` (enforced by `npm run check:kiro`).
- [ ] URL to the working app (https://main.d1dlm8l72zqe87.amplifyapp.com). Judge login credentials go only in the private Devpost form.
- [ ] Text description of features and functionality.
- [ ] Demo video under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, showing the app working; no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music without permission.
- [ ] Kiro write-up covering: IDE/CLI/Web/Crew; custom agents; agent hooks; spec-driven development (vs vibe coding); steering strategy; MCP; property-based testing; Kiro powers. Draw from `docs/kiro-usage-log.md`.

## Bonus prizes worth entering
- **Best Demo:** automatic for all eligible submissions.
- **Bonus Blog Prize** (2,000 Kiro credits, 100 winners): public blog post about Kiro and the project, mentioning Kiroween 2026, published during the period.
- **Social Blitz Prize:** social post starting with `#KiroweenHackathon26` about what changed with Kiro.
- Not eligible as far as we know: Best Startup, Best Student, Ghost of Kiroween Past (needs a 2025 registration), Best Kiro Crew App.
- One project can win one Overall or one Category prize, plus any number of bonus prizes.
